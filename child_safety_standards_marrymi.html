Child Safety Standards — Marrymi

Marrymi is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We maintain a strict zero‑tolerance policy for any form of CSAE or CSAM (child sexual abuse material).

Our Commitments

  • No user may upload, share, or request CSAM or exploitative content.
  • We review and remove any reported or detected CSAE content immediately.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and child protection authorities when necessary.

Reporting

If you find content or behavior involving CSAE, report it through email us at contact@airlabs.in. In emergencies, contact your local law enforcement first.

Review

This policy is reviewed regularly to align with best practices and legal requirements. Last updated: 10-27-2025
